## Phase 423~426 — Boss Reward Clarity

## Phase 427~430 — Shop Build Guidance

- Added `src/game/shop-guidance.ts`.
- Uses current hero, resolved build archetype, equipment state, and rank to score the offers already generated by `generateShopOffers()`.
- At most two cards are emphasized; completed rank-5 equipment is never recommended.
- Existing price, reroll, purchase, legendary evolution, and inventory behavior is untouched.
- Recommendations recompute immediately after purchase and reroll.

## Phase 431~434 — Build Recovery Guidance

- Added `src/game/build-recovery-guidance.ts`.
- Silent before 10 minutes to avoid tutorial noise during the opening.
- Reuses the existing build HUD and shows at most one `RECOVER` row.
- Recovery priority: missing equipment slot → first relic → nearest incomplete fusion path.
- If a fusion is already ready, the line points to the next boss reward; otherwise it names the limiting hero-specific spell and target Lv.10.
- Structurally complete builds produce no generic advice line.

## Phase 435~438 — AUTO Targeting Feel

- Added `src/game/auto-targeting.ts`.
- AUTO-only score considers direct core threats, bosses/elites, dangerous specialists, distance, and bounded low-HP cleanup value.
- Auto priority range is bounded to 720 logical px and ties are deterministic by enemy id.
- Manual normal-spell targeting and manual ultimate targeting preserve the previous core-attacker → elite/boss → nearest policy.
- No new AUTO modes or settings were added.

## Phase 439~442 — Long-Run HUD Focus

- Added `src/game/long-run-hud-focus.ts`.
- 2/4/8-hour tiers progressively reduce optional status/build text.
- HP/XP/meter bars, core bar, danger semantics, and 9 action buttons remain mandatory.
- Boss/Mythic state further reduces optional labels without touching collision, enemy logic, telegraphs, or combat pressure.
- Active meter text can still surface even when passive long-run meter text is hidden.
