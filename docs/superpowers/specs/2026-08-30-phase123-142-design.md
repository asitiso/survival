# Phase 123-142 Combat Feel & Mobile Finish Design

## Goal
Polish the existing endless defense loop without adding combat actions or management screens.

## Constraints
- Keep the 9 action contract unchanged.
- Reuse existing boss arena, Final Form, director, HUD, and mobile input systems.
- Prefer bounded pure modules over new persistent subsystems.
- Preserve deterministic/snapshot-compatible long-run behavior.
- Enemy AI/spawn capability must not be sacrificed before presentation density.

## Phase 123-126 — Boss Arena Geometry
Mythic archetypes receive distinct geometry descriptors (ring, brood pockets, iron corridor, void orbit, twin cross, broken clock). Geometry modifies hazard placement and rendering, while the existing mutation modifiers continue to own numeric pressure. Destroyed weakpoints soften geometry pressure.

## Phase 127-130 — Final Form Flow Combo
Final Form movement families gain a short, automatic flow combo from moving and casting. The combo is transient gameplay state, not a new permanent progression system. No button is added; spell casts while moving build a capped streak that briefly improves damage/cooldown according to movement family.

## Phase 131-134 — Long-Run Fatigue Relief
After two hours, presentation gradually becomes calmer: fewer simultaneous build labels, less decorative VFX density, and slower noncritical notification cadence. Danger telegraphs and combat difficulty remain intact.

## Phase 135-138 — First Ten Minute Combat Pacing
A bounded opening director adds three readable pacing bands: ignition (0-2m), momentum (2-5m), escalation (5-10m). It slightly increases early spawn/elite pressure and reward tempo without changing the established first shop or mobile entity cap.

## Phase 139-142 — Landscape HUD Final Pass
HUD priority is explicit: critical combat state > replay/active power > build history. Mythic/boss combat reduces noncritical label count, while normal play keeps up to four lines. Compact status text preserves map, Threat, and danger first.

## Verification
- New unit tests for all five modules.
- Integration tests assert no new ActionId or result-screen control.
- Full regression and TypeScript build.
- HTTP smoke for production dist modules.
